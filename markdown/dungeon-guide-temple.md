## Entry and run structure

Temple of the Last Rite ranges from difficulty 2,200 to 3,300 and provides reagents with several different uses. Fire resistance addresses Desert Sun in the opening half; Poison resistance protects against Jax the Stinger.

Unlock and availability: Temple dungeon research; currently hold Orcish Horde stage 4. Entry supplies: 220 Crowns. Availability depends on currently holding the required war progress, not merely having held it once.

Send five Adventurers. All eight stages have the default 10 Energy completion cost, for 80 Energy before hazards, limit breaks, and recovery. The party is cleared at the end of a run rather than returned as an output.

## Preparation and encounter notes

Crystallax uses two physical direct strikes: Brutal Slam and Flailing Strike. The Embalming Chamber and Inner Sanctum use a group Magic trap check. Amun the Orchestrator adds Tremors to tank-targeted shadow damage.

Sholrath’s Chosen combines Consuming Shadows with Tank Shadow Damage. Shadow resistance helps the Wisdom-failed party hazard, but the tank-targeted shadow challenge omits resistance multiplication in this implementation.

## Full clear rewards

465 Ducats; 12 Toxin Samples; 90 Mercurial Flux; 14 Symbiote Reagents; 6 Dark Essence. Full-run base totals before upgrades or trash procs.
