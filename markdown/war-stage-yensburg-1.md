# Artisan Row

Stage 1 of Siege of Yensburg.

## Description

The world-renowned crafters in Artisan Row have brought business to Yensburg for generations. Now, their workshops supply weapons and armor for the enemy.

## Reward Description

Retake Artisan Row to unlock the Inventor tech!
