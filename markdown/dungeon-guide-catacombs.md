## Entry and run structure

Yensburg Catacombs ranges from difficulty 3,200 to 5,000. Its first approach is difficulty 3,500 while its first boss is 3,200; the sequence is not strictly increasing.

Unlock and availability: Catacombs research; currently hold Siege of Yensburg stage 3. Entry supplies: 320 Crowns. Availability depends on currently holding the required war progress, not merely having held it once.

Send five Adventurers. All eight stages have the default 10 Energy completion cost, for 80 Energy before hazards, limit breaks, and recovery. The party is cleared at the end of a run rather than returned as an output.

## Preparation and encounter notes

Uzgrim the Hermit combines Magic-checked Mana Poison with tank Fire damage. Bone Golem uses ordinary armor-sensitive strikes. Guardian of the Ninth Gate adds Dexterity-checked Pyroclasm to tank Fire damage, making party Fire resistance useful beyond just the tank.

Mausoleum of Heroes has constant Exhausting pressure. The final Sanctum tests Magic; Herald of the Legion combines tank Fire damage and Reaping Strike. Maintaining health and energy across all eight stages matters as much as the final boss.

## Full clear rewards

585 Ducats; 6 Demonic Writings. Full-run base totals before upgrades or trash procs.
