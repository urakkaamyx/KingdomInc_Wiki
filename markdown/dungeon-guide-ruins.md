## Entry and run structure

Ruins of Arlingen is the first dungeon. Its difficulty rises from 1,000 to 2,000 across four approaches and four bosses. Strength, Magic, Dexterity, Fire protection, and energy all have distinct uses here.

Unlock and availability: Dungeon research; currently hold Orcish Horde stage 2. Entry supplies: 210 Rings. Availability depends on currently holding the required war progress, not merely having held it once.

Send five Adventurers. All eight stages have the default 10 Energy completion cost, for 80 Energy before hazards, limit breaks, and recovery. The party is cleared at the end of a run rather than returned as an output.

## Preparation and encounter notes

Crull the Defiler combines tank-targeted Fire damage with party-wide Acid Rain. Quartermaster Vadric tests Wisdom through Soul Reave while striking the tank. Black Eye Marlowe can be stopped by a successful group Strength check. The final Watcher of Tomorrows combines the code’s Tank Shadow Damage exception with Dexterity-checked Scatter Beam.

Solitary Confinement adds Exhausting every active tick, on top of the eight default stage costs. A tank/healer composition reduces ordinary damage pressure, but it does not replace the separate skill checks.

## Full clear rewards

250 Ducats; 52 Ancient Gems; 8 Observer Eyes. These are full-run base totals, excluding coin upgrades, trash procs, and quality variation.
