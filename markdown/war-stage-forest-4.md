# Yensmouth

Stage 4 of Southside Brigands.

## Description

The famous port town of Yensmouth was once the home of the kingdom's Adventurer's Guild. However, with access cut off by the Southside Brigands, it has languished in obscurity for years.

## Reward Description

Retake Yensmouth to begin restoring the Adventurer's Guild!

## Reward Complete

Adventurer's Guild unlocked!
