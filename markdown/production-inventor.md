# Inventors, experiments, and Ingenuity

The Inventor converts materials into parts, research currency, and advanced equipment.

## Tinkering and directed production

Tinker consumes one arcanium ingot, one crystal mana, and one attuned catalyst. With its twofold time multiplier, its default craft time is six seconds. It chooses one Widget, Gizmo, or Doodad with equal configured weights of 0.3333 and gives a base reward of 12 Ingenuity.

Direct part recipes have no item reagents and cost 28 Ingenuity for Widget, 22 for Gizmo, or 26 for Doodad. Their default craft time is 1.5 seconds before building Speed. Completing the Widget recipe triggers Widget Inc.

## Experiments

Mechanical Experiment consumes a widget and two iron ingots for a base reward of 16 Ingenuity. Alchemical Experiment consumes a gizmo and soothing herbs for 14. Biological Experiment consumes a doodad and stalker eye for 16.

Experiments have no item outputs. Currency rewards follow the normal recipe completion formula: reward base × rolled output quality, rounded. They can therefore operate without an item-output pipe.

## Advanced material and equipment chain

Inventor combines arcanium ingot and widget into tinkersteel, embroidered cotton and gizmo into witchcloth, and embossed leather and doodad into primal leather. Petrified wood uses resin, attuned catalyst, ancient wood plank, and 4 Ingenuity.

Further recipes spend Ingenuity on winged boots, golem gauntlets, goggles, chainsaw, shield arm, and triune shield. Nightmare Collector consumes nightmare casing, cleansed nightmare, and two decoded writings plus 257 Ingenuity, with a fivefold time multiplier.
