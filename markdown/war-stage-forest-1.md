# Byrgenwald

Stage 1 of Southside Brigands.

## Description

The ancient forest of Byrgenwald has supplied Yensburg for many generations. Its massive trees provide the highest quality lumber, and the hunters that predate on the forest's many animals feed the hungry mouths in the city.

## Reward Description

Retake the forest to unlock the Forest tech!

## Reward Complete
