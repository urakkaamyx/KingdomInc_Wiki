## Entry and run structure

Tomb of Sholrath has base difficulties 6,400–10,000. Its eight stages alternate BaseHealth 50 approaches with BaseHealth 40 bosses. Active Nightmare scaling increases difficulty, health, and entry cost.

Unlock and availability: Endgame dungeon research; currently hold Ironcrag Siege stage 4. Entry supplies: 380 Ducats before Nightmare scaling. Availability depends on currently holding the required war progress, not merely having held it once.

Send five Adventurers. All eight stages have the default 10 Energy completion cost, for 80 Energy before hazards, limit breaks, and recovery. The party is cleared at the end of a run rather than returned as an output.

## Preparation and encounter notes

Belgor the Infernal combines Pyroclasm and tank Fire damage. The Last High Priest combines Desert Sun and Magic-checked Mana Poison. Herald of Sholrath uses Consuming Shadows and a Wisdom check that can drain fixed 30 Energy.

Tendril of Sholrath combines the Tank Shadow Damage resistance exception with Waking Nightmare, which drains energy and deals Shadow-scaled armor-ignoring damage to every party member. Shadow protection remains valuable for Waking Nightmare even though the separate tank strike omits it.

## Full clear rewards

10,250 Faith across the first seven stages. The final stage conditionally grants 2 Fragmented Nightmares and 1 Distilled Nightmare progression; see Nightmare reward readiness. Base totals exclude currency multipliers.
