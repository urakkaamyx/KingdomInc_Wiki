# Castle District

Stage 3 of Siege of Yensburg.

## Description

The wealthy Castle District is home to Yensburg's nobility, with the former king's grand castle at its center. Evidence shows that the enemy infiltrated the city from this place.

## Reward Description

Retake the Castle District to unlock the Yensburg Catacombs dungeon!

## Reward Complete

Yensburg Catacombs unlocked!
