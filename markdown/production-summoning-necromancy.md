# Summoning and necromancy

Mana and Faith recipes create combatants or transform existing adventurers.

## Summoning Circle

Summon Gremlin spends 320 Mana with no item reagents. Summon Hellspawn spends 680 Mana and a gem topaz. Summon Observer spends 390 Mana and an observer eye. These summoning recipes use a crafting-time multiplier of 8.

Faith summons are Cherub for 124 Faith with no item reagents, Valkyrie for 290 Faith plus gold armor, and Arbiter for 475 Faith plus an arcanium greatsword, also at an eightfold time multiplier.

## Necromancer

Necromancer consumes buried remains to create undead. Shambler costs one buried remains and 460 Mana. Skeletal Archer adds a softwood bow and costs 520 Mana. Skeletal Berserker adds an arcanium ingot and costs 550 Mana. Skeletal Mage adds a gem ruby and costs 990 Mana.

Hulking Monstrosity consumes three buried remains and enchanted cotton thread plus 2,480 Mana. All five undead creation recipes use an eightfold crafting-time multiplier.

## Class transformations

Demonologist uses a Warlock, blank tome, and gremlin at the Summoning Circle. Necrolyte uses a Warlock, blank tome, and buried remains at the Necromancer. These recipes use a fourfold crafting-time multiplier and BaseProductivity −999.

TransformAdventurer changes the existing human item's type and adds 0.05 training through AddTraining. It preserves the existing item and its character data instead of creating a new independently generated recruit.
