# Ruins of Arlingen

Stage 2 of Orcish Horde.

## Description

In ancient times these ruins were home to a large fortress, treasury, and prison complex. As the territory of Yensburg expanded the location was abandoned, and eventually overtaken by forces with nefarious intent.

Retake the ruins to begin your descent into the Dungeon!
