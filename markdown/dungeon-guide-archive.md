## Entry and run structure

Timeless Archive ranges from difficulty 5,000 to 6,600. Boss BaseHealth is 40 rather than the 30 used by earlier dungeons, so equal difficulty would already produce a longer fight.

Unlock and availability: Archive research; currently hold Ironcrag Siege stage 1. Entry supplies: 180 Ducats. Availability depends on currently holding the required war progress, not merely having held it once.

Send five Adventurers. All eight stages have the default 10 Energy completion cost, for 80 Energy before hazards, limit breaks, and recovery. The party is cleared at the end of a run rather than returned as an output.

## Preparation and encounter notes

Champion of the Vault mixes a tank strike with a random target’s Dexterity check. Cannaan the Librarian tests each adventurer’s Strength through Muscle Magic. Pelonnar the Dragonlord combines party Acid Rain and a group Wisdom energy check.

Private Collection has Animated Guardians at a 50% hazard chance each combat tick. The Mad Archivist combines Wisdom-checked Consuming Shadows with random energy extraction. A party with several useful skill stats avoids relying only on its main damage stat.

## Full clear rewards

585 Ducats; 6 Heretic Technologies. Full-run base totals before upgrades or trash procs.
