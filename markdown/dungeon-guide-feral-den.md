# Feral Den — expedition guide

Feral Den ranges from difficulty 1,400 to 2,900. Poison resistance and energy recovery are especially relevant to its later stages.

## Entry and run structure

Feral Den ranges from difficulty 1,400 to 2,900. Poison resistance and energy recovery are especially relevant to its later stages.

Unlock and availability: Feral dungeon research; currently hold Southside Brigands stage 3. Entry supplies: 320 Rings. Availability depends on currently holding the required war progress, not merely having held it once.

Send five Adventurers. All eight stages have the default 10 Energy completion cost, for 80 Energy before hazards, limit breaks, and recovery. The party is cleared at the end of a run rather than returned as an output.

## Preparation and encounter notes

Contaminated Aquifer and Patient Zero use Major Poison, whose installed implementation hits the first party member. Patient Zero also uses a group Wisdom check through Chilling Howl. Gnawtooth the Toxiferous targets the tank with Inject Poison and randomly drains energy through Extract Vigor.

Any positive Poison reduction prevents the initial Inject Poison debuff from being added. Its later damage ticks bypass armor. Strength helps with the Den Entrance rubble and Alpha Beast charge; Dexterity helps with poison avoidance and Ratling Burrows tremors.

## Full clear rewards

350 Ducats; 130 Unidentified Plants; 32 Toxin Samples. Full-run base totals before upgrades or trash procs.
